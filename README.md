## Evidence and disclosure

Implementation repositories may remain private. Published materials describe reviewed architecture, technology, controls, tests, deployment evidence, and known limitations without exposing customer data, credentials, or proprietary source code.

**Implementation is not validation. Validation is not production readiness.** Each case specifies its own evidence and maturity boundaries. Published snapshots may be closed independently of ongoing software development.

The portfolio documents selected projects and does not, by itself, establish a complete chronology of all professional work.
